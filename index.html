What Musiconda does

Pick a song and Musiconda breaks it down for you, in real time.

Key detection
Find the musical key a song is written in.
Tempo (BPM)
See how fast a track really is, down to the beat.
Chord recognition
Get the chord progression pulled straight from the audio.
Live chord sync
Watch chords change on screen in time with playback.

How to install

Musiconda isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android needs a moment of your trust.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the download button near the top of this page.

  2. Open the downloaded file

    Find it in your notifications or your Downloads folder, then tap it to open.

  3. Allow installation from this source

    Android will warn you it's not from the Play Store — tap "Settings," then turn on "Allow from this source" and go back to install.

  4. Install and open

    Tap install, wait a few seconds, then open Musiconda from your app list.

How to use it

From a song to its chords in four steps.

  1. Paste a link or search

    Drop in a YouTube link, or search for a song by name.

  2. Select the song

    Choose the right result from the list.

  3. Let it analyze

    Musiconda processes the audio on your device — this can take a moment for longer songs.

  4. Play

    Hit play and watch the key, BPM, and chords update live as the song runs.

Before you install

A few things worth knowing about this beta.

Your audio stays on your phone

  • All analysis happens locally, on-device.
  • Musiconda doesn't upload your songs, your listening history, or any audio to a server.

Known limitations

  • This is an early community beta — expect rough edges.
  • Some songs may fail to analyze, especially heavily produced, orchestral, or very noisy tracks.
  • Chord and key detection can be less accurate in dense or fast-changing passages.
  • Battery and processing time will vary by device and song length.
